# FinRise PDF Toolbox - Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 - November 2026. Provided by Devendra Choudhary ("we").
Contact: hr@finriseadvisors.com

## In short

* **Your documents never leave your computer.** Every PDF tool runs locally.
* **No tracking, no analytics, no advertising profiles.**
* The app only downloads a small update file; it does not send your details.

## What we collect and why

| Information | Where it comes from | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, email address, firm (optional) | You, when you ask for a licence key or in the installer's registration page | To issue and send your licence key, and to support you | Our licence records; and on your computer in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FinRisePdfToolbox` |
| Licence key details (licence ID, plan, end date, name/email/firm written into the key, and the computer ID if the key is locked to a computer) | We create them when we issue your key | To check that the app is licensed and to cancel keys that are misused | Our licence records; and encrypted on your computer |
| Computer ID | Calculated by the app from Windows' machine identifier (a one-way code, not your name or files) | Only if you send it to us, so we can lock a key to your computer | On your computer; in our records only if you send it |

The installer's registration details stay on your computer. They are only sent to
us if you choose "Request key by email", which opens an email in your own mail
program for you to review and send.

## What the app does online

* **Update check:** at start-up the app downloads `update.json` from GitHub
  (https://github.com/devendrachoudhary55-hash/finrise-pdf-toolbox-releases). No
  information about you or your documents is sent. Like any website, GitHub can
  see your IP address and that the app connected; see GitHub's privacy statement.
* **Updates:** when you click "Update now" the installer is downloaded from the same
  place.
* **If a free version with ads is offered in future:** ad text and pictures may be
  loaded from the advertiser's https address, which can see your IP address.
  Clicking an ad opens your web browser. No personal information is shared.
* **If automatic subscription renewal is offered in future:** the app sends your
  licence ID and computer ID to our licence server to fetch a renewed key.

## On your computer

The app keeps your licence key (encrypted for your Windows user), its last update
check, and a small diagnostic log (`app.log`, which records what the app did, never
your documents' contents) in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FinRisePdfToolbox`. Uninstalling the app
does not delete this folder; you can delete it yourself.

## How long we keep your information

While your licence is active, and afterwards as long as needed for accounting,
tax and legal purposes, after which it is deleted.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and, where applicable,
other data-protection laws such as the GDPR) you may ask to access, correct or
delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. Email hr@finriseadvisors.com. If you
ask us to delete your data, your licence key can no longer be supported or
re-issued.

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only if the law requires us
to.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the current version ships with each release of the app.
